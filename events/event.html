About this event

Join a curated group of Liverpool SME owners, marketers and hospitality operators for a relaxed Sunday afternoon lunch & learn. We will unpack what actually works for local venues in 2026 — from loyalty programmes and door-to-door conversion, to how the latest booking tools are reshaping weekend trade.

Expect a light two-course lunch, one short talk from an industry guest, and a structured networking round where everyone leaves with at least three warm introductions. You can also join remotely via our livestream and breakout rooms.

What's included

  • Two-course seasonal lunch & unlimited soft drinks
  • 30-minute talk from a guest speaker (announced one week prior)
  • Facilitated networking with name badges & intro cards
  • Digital attendee list with opt-in contact sharing
  • Livestream access for remote attendees, with breakout rooms
