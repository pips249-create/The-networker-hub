    Before you start

    Listing is free. We will walk you through four short steps — here is what you need first.

    1. 1 Sign in or create a free account You need a Hub account to manage your listing.
    2. 2 Create your organiser page Your networking group or business name, logo, and a short description.
    3. 3 Event format & details In person or online, dates, venue, description, and cover image.
    4. 4 Tickets & publish Set ticket types and prices, then submit for approval.

    Have ready: date and time, venue or online link, ticket prices, and a cover image.
