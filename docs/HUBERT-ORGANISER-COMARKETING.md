# Hubert × organiser co-marketing pack

**Purpose:** Help event hosts promote Hubert Matchmaker to guests **~48 hours before** an event, so attendees arrive with warm intros underway.

**When to use:** Soft launch / after Matchmaker is on for members. Do not blast this while Matchmaker is admin-only.

---

## Organiser value prop (one liner)

We give your guests an AI matchmaker so they meet the right people at your event — higher satisfaction, more repeat tickets.

---

## Suggested send window

- **48 hours before** the event start (or the evening before for breakfast sessions).
- Audience: booked / registered attendees with a valid email.
- Prefer the organiser’s own “from” address when they forward copy; Hub can also send as branded mail when tooling is ready.

---

## Email — subject

`Who do you want to meet at {{event_day}}’s meetup?`

Examples:
- `Who do you want to meet at Thursday’s meetup?`
- `Who do you want to meet at Manchester Founders Breakfast?`

---

## Email — body (copy-paste)

Hi {{first_name}},

You’re booked for **{{event_name}}**. We’ve partnered with Hubert on The Networker UK to help you get warm introductions before you arrive.

Log in, tell Hubert who you’re looking for, and start connecting:

{{meet_people_url}}  
(e.g. https://thenetworkeruk.com/account/#meet)

Intros stay on The Networker UK — email, phone, and LinkedIn are never shared via Hubert.

See you there,  
{{organiser_name}}

## Guardrails

- Same privacy line everywhere: **no contact dump**.
- Don’t promise guest-list browsing.
- Soft launch first with 2–3 friendly organisers; measure clicks → Meet people → searches → intro requests.
