# Organiser data-sharing schedule (optional template)

**Between:** The Networker Group Ltd (“**Platform**”) and [Organiser legal name] (“**Organiser**”)  
**Product:** The Networker Hub  
**Last updated:** 10 July 2026

*Optional one-page schedule for enterprise organisers who request written terms. **Not required** for standard self-serve listing — organiser terms section 6 already covers roles. Not legal advice — review with your solicitor before signing.*

---

## 1. Purpose

This schedule describes how attendee and enquiry personal data flows when the Organiser uses the Platform to list events or business opportunities. It does **not** make the Platform the Organiser’s data processor for event delivery.

---

## 2. Roles (UK GDPR)

| Party | Role | Scope |
|-------|------|--------|
| **The Networker Group Ltd** | **Data controller** | Member accounts, platform operation, checkout technology, email orchestration, fraud prevention, complaints |
| **Organiser** | **Separate data controller** | Attendee/enquiry data received to run events or respond to opportunities |

This is a **controller-to-controller** sharing arrangement, not an Article 28 processor agreement.

---

## 3. Data shared with Organiser

When an attendee books or enquires, the Platform may share:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Attendee name and email | Event administration, communication about the booking |
| Guest names (if provided) | Check-in and attendance |
| Ticket type, quantity, payment status | Fulfilment (card data stays with Stripe) |
| Application answers (if applicable) | Event admission decisions |
| Enquiry message and contact details | Opportunity follow-up |

The Platform does not authorise the Organiser to use this data for unrelated marketing without separate consent.

---

## 4. Organiser obligations

The Organiser shall:

1. Use shared data only for the relevant event or opportunity  
2. Comply with UK GDPR and PECR  
3. Keep data secure and limit internal access  
4. Honour data subject rights for data the Organiser holds  
5. Not sell or disclose data to third parties except as needed to run the event (e.g. venue staff) under equivalent safeguards  
6. Delete or anonymise data when no longer needed, subject to legal retention  
7. Notify the Platform at **hello@thenetworkerhub.com** within 24 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting shared data  

---

## 5. Platform obligations

The Platform shall:

1. Share only data necessary for the booking or enquiry  
2. Maintain appropriate technical and organisational security (see `docs/RoPA.md`)  
3. Process payments via Stripe under separate Stripe terms  
4. Suspend listings that misuse personal data  

---

## 6. Term and termination

This schedule starts on the date signed and continues while the Organiser maintains an active listing, unless terminated earlier.

On termination, the Organiser must delete or anonymise Hub-sourced personal data unless retention is required by law, and confirm deletion on request.
