Policies Organiser terms

Event listing agreement

Short-form agreement for listing FTT Fintech Festival on The Networker Hub. Full platform rules sit in our published Organiser Terms.

1. Parties

This agreement is between The Networker Group Ltd (company no. 15252227) trading as The Networker Hub (“Hub”) and VC Innovations Ltd (“Organiser”).

2. Purpose

The Hub will list the Organiser’s event FTT Fintech Festival (9–10 November 2026, The Brewery, London) on thenetworkerhub.com, and may create a public organiser profile for VC Innovations / Fintech Talents.

3. Listing options

  • Option A (Discovery): Directory listing with link to the Organiser’s chosen registration URL. No Hub ticket sale.
  • Option B (Hub ticketing): Tickets sold via Hub Stripe Connect. Organiser receives 100% of the ticket price set. Attendees pay a booking fee of 4.5% + 20p per ticket at checkout.

The Organiser confirms Option A or B in writing (email is fine). Option A is the default if unspecified.

4. Fees

  • Creating and maintaining the organiser page and Festival listing is free.
  • Optional paid placements (e.g. Premium Spotlight, sponsorship) are separate and only apply if the Organiser later requests them in writing.
  • Under Option B only, the attendee booking fee applies as published on the Hub.

5. Content and brand

  • The Organiser warrants that listing copy, images and ticket information are accurate and that it has the right to use submitted logos and materials.
  • The Organiser grants the Hub a non-exclusive licence to display those materials for the listing and related Hub communications about the event.
  • Listings are not an endorsement by the Hub of the Organiser, its speakers, sponsors or exhibitors.

6. Responsibilities

  • Organiser: delivers the event; manages its own registration/ticket fulfilment under Option A; complies with applicable law and the Hub’s published Organiser Terms.
  • Hub: provides the directory listing and organiser tools in good faith; does not guarantee ticket volume, attendance or commercial outcomes.

7. Data

Under Option A, registration data stays on the Organiser’s systems. Under Option B, booking data is processed as described in the Hub Privacy Policy and Organiser Terms (attendee details shared with the Organiser to run the booking).

8. Term and ending

Starts when both parties confirm by email (or signed below) and continues until the Festival ends, or either party ends the listing earlier by written notice. The Hub may remove a listing that breaches Organiser Terms or is inaccurate.

9. Liability

Nothing in this agreement excludes liability that cannot be limited under UK law. Subject to that, each party’s total liability arising from this listing arrangement is limited to £500, except for unpaid Option B ticket amounts owed to the Organiser.

10. Governing law

England and Wales. Related platform use is also governed by the Hub Terms and Organiser Terms at thenetworkerhub.com/legal-policies.
