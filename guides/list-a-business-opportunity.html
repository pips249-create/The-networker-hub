List a business opportunity

Publish a franchise, partnership, side hustle, or other opportunity and receive enquiries from the hub community.

← All guides

  1. Open the organiser dashboard

    Sign in and go to Business opportunities in the sidebar. You need an organiser account — the same workspace you use for events.

  2. Start a new listing

    Choose List a listing. You’ll see an overview of how listings work, pricing, and what happens after submission — then open the listing form.

  3. Complete the listing form

    Add the information prospects need to understand your offer:

    • Title and opportunity type (franchise, partnership, distributorship, etc.)
    • Summary and full description
    • Investment or pricing notes, location, and industry where relevant
    • Images and contact details
    • Link to an organiser page if the opportunity is tied to your networking group

    Listings are reviewed before going live. Keep claims factual — the hub does not provide financial advice or endorse specific opportunities.

  4. Submit for review

    Submit the listing. You’ll receive email updates when it is approved, live, or if changes are needed. While pending, you can edit from your dashboard.

  5. Optional: premium placement

    Featured carousel slots may be available for extra visibility on the opportunities browse page. If offered, you can upgrade from your dashboard after submission — availability is limited.

  6. Manage enquiries

    When someone enquires, you receive an email notification. Reply directly to the prospect from your inbox. Track new enquiries from Business opportunities → Overview in the dashboard.

  7. Renew or update

    Listings run for a set period. You’ll get reminder emails before expiry. Renew or edit the listing from your dashboard to stay visible on Browse opportunities.
