Steps to list

  1. Open the organiser dashboard

    Sign in and go to Business opportunities in the sidebar. You need an organiser account — the same workspace you use for events.

  2. Start a new listing

    Choose List a listing. You’ll see an overview of how listings work, pricing, and what happens after submission — then open the listing form.

  3. Complete the listing form

    Add the information prospects need to understand your offer:

    • Title and opportunity type (franchise, partnership, distributorship / reseller rights, etc.)
    • Summary and full description
    • Investment or pricing notes, location, and industry where relevant
    • Images and contact details
    • Link to an organiser page if the opportunity is tied to your networking group

    Listings are reviewed before payment. Keep claims factual — The Networker UK does not provide financial advice or endorse specific opportunities.

  4. Submit for review

    Submit the listing — there is no charge at this stage. You’ll get an email confirming it is pending review. While pending, you can edit from your dashboard.

  5. Pay after approval to go live

    When we approve your listing, you’ll receive an email with a Stripe link. Start the monthly subscription (£25 + VAT) and the listing goes live on the directory immediately.

  6. Optional: premium placement

    After your listing is live, you can add an optional £55 one-time Premium Spotlight for up to 30 days (maximum 12 in the carousel at once). Buy it from My business opportunities — it is not a subscription. Details: Featured Opportunity Boost.

  7. Manage enquiries

    When someone enquires, you receive an email notification. Reply directly to the prospect from your inbox. Track new enquiries from Business opportunities → Overview in the dashboard.

  8. Renew or update

    The directory listing is a monthly Stripe subscription (£25 + VAT). Cancel any time from your listing page via Manage or cancel subscription. To stay in the Premium Spotlight after 30 days, buy another one-time boost. Edit the listing from your dashboard — copy or image changes go live after a quick review. Browse: business opportunities.
