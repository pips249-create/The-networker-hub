What happens when you start

  1. Sign in to the organiser dashboard

    Sign in and open Organiser dashboard (/organiser/). If your networking group is already listed, claim your organiser page when prompted (use the email address we have on file for your group).

  2. Create or claim your organiser page

    On Overview, follow the setup checklist — add your public group profile (name, logo, description, contact email, and social links) or confirm the claim prompt for an existing page.

  3. Optional: set up membership

    If you sell members-only tickets or track renewals, open Memberships and upload your member register. You can skip this and come back later.

  4. List your event

    From My events, choose List event. Hubert guides you through format, details, tickets, and publish. For paid tickets, connect Stripe under the Revenue tab before you publish.

    Have ready: date and time, venue or online link, ticket prices, and a cover image.

Hosting a conference or exhibition? See the conference & exhibition guide for delegate tiers and multi-day series.

Questions while you work? Ask Hubert in the dashboard sidebar, or email hi@thenetworkeruk.com.
