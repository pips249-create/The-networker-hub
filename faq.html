FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about browsing events, your account, and listing as an organiser. Can’t find what you need? Get in touch.

  • What is The Networker Hub?

    The Networker Hub is a UK platform for discovering networking meetings, exhibitions, conferences, and professional training. Members can search and book; organisers can list events and reach new audiences.

  • How do I find events?

    Use Browse events in the top navigation to open the events listing. You can filter by type, date, industry, and location as more filters are enabled on the site.

  • Do I need an account to browse?

    No — you can explore listings without signing in. Creating an account lets you sign in across devices, access member features as they launch, and (for admins) open the Admin panel.

  • How do I create an account or sign in?

    Click Sign in in the navigation and enter the email and password provided when your account was set up. If you forgot your password, use the link on the sign-in page to reset it.

  • How do I book a ticket?

    Open an event from the listing page for full details, pricing, and booking options. Booking flows are being expanded; some events may link to an external ticket page or organiser contact while we complete integrated checkout.

  • I’m an organiser — how do I list an event?

    We’re onboarding organisers in phases. Email hello@the-networker.co.uk with your group name, typical event format, and location so we can set up your profile and listings.

  • Where does event information come from?

    Listings are managed in our events database and updated by organisers and the Networker Hub team. Dates, venues, and prices on each event page are the source of truth for that event.

  • What is The Academy?

    The Academy is the training side of the hub — workshops, seminars, webinars, and masterclasses. You’ll see more Academy listings on the homepage and in search as we publish them.

  • Who operates the site?

    The Networker Hub is operated by The Networker Group Ltd. See our About us page for more on our mission and services.
