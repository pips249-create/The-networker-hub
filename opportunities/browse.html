FAQs

Browsing opportunities

Is it free to browse?

Yes — searching, filtering and enquiring on listings is completely free. You only pay if you decide to list your own opportunity.

How do I enquire about a listing?

Click Enquire on any card to contact the poster directly. There are no middlemen and no per-lead fees.

What types of opportunity are listed?

Franchises, networking groups, side hustles, referral partnerships, white-label arrangements, distributorships and other business opportunities across the UK.

Are listings verified?

All submissions are manually reviewed before going live. We check for completeness and legitimacy to protect both posters and enquirers.
