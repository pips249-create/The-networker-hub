FAQs

Listing an opportunity

How quickly will my listing go live?

Submit for review first (no charge). We typically review within one working day. Once approved, you pay via Stripe and the listing goes live immediately.

What exactly does the £25 buy?

A standard directory listing: your opportunity appears on the opportunities browse and search results and has a public detail page where signed-in members can enquire. It stays live while your monthly subscription is active. It is not homepage placement, email/newsletter inclusion, social promotion, or Premium Spotlight — those are separate or not included.

Is £25 the same as Premium Spotlight?

No. £25/month + VAT is the standard directory listing. Featured Opportunity Boost (Premium Spotlight) is optional, £55 one-time for up to 30 days of pinned/highlighted placement on the opportunities browse page. It is never applied automatically — see /advertising.

How do enquiries reach me?

A signed-in member submits an enquiry from your opportunity page. You receive the enquiry details needed to respond. The Networker facilitates the message; it does not sell or retain the lead as a brokered product, and it does not guarantee outcomes.

Can I edit or remove my listing?

Yes — update or unpublish any time from your dashboard. Cancelling the Stripe subscription stops future renewals; unused days are generally not refunded once the listing has been published.

What types of opportunity can I post?

Anything business-related: franchise territories, side hustle models, referral partnerships, white-label arrangements, distributorships, and more.

How much does a listing cost?

Listings are £25 per month + VAT as a monthly Stripe subscription (cancel any time). That is the complete cost of the standard directory listing — no automatic upgrades, renewals beyond the monthly subscription you started, or promotional fees unless you separately choose them (for example Featured Opportunity Boost at £55 one-time).

What if my listing is not approved?

You are not charged until after approval. If we cannot approve a listing, we email you the reason so you can edit and resubmit. Fees for published premium placements are generally non-refundable once published, except where required by law.

Is there a free option?

Browsing and enquiring is free for members. Business opportunity listings require the paid listing fee above — paid securely via Stripe to The Networker UK.

Do you verify opportunities?

We do not verify or endorse investment opportunities. Before publication we check that each submission is complete, that only one listing exists per company, and that wording is not misleading — this is not financial advice or FCA-regulated approval. Always conduct your own due diligence.
