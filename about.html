About us

Built for people who grow business through connection

The Networker Hub is your central place to discover UK networking meetings, exhibitions, and business opportunities — and the home for organisers who run them.

Who runs the hub

The Networker Hub is run by Rosie and Catherine — the team behind The Networker Group Ltd. Together they built the hub to connect UK business owners and professionals with the right events, communities, and opportunities.

Our mission

We believe the right room at the right time changes careers and companies. The Networker Hub brings together event listings, organiser pages, and business opportunities in one trusted platform so members can find what matters locally and nationally, and organisers can reach the audiences they deserve.

What we offer

  • Events & exhibitions — breakfast networking, mixers, conferences, and trade shows in one searchable listing.
  • Organiser pages — see who runs your local business community and explore everything they host.
  • Business opportunities — franchises, partnerships, and deals for members exploring their next move.

The Networker Hub is operated by The Networker Group Ltd. We work with organisers across the UK to list quality networking and professional development opportunities.

For organisers

If you run networking meetings and want to list on the hub, sell tickets, and manage attendees, we’d love to hear from you. Listing and onboarding support is available as we roll out full organiser tools.
