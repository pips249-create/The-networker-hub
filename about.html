Our story

From a UK directory to a fuller platform

Meetings Webinars Exhibitions Funding Directories Groups

2023

One place to look

Networking was spread thinly across the web: face-to-face meetings, online sessions, exhibitions, and funding opportunities sat on different sites. The Networker launched as a UK directory and regional marketplace so people could find them in one place.

Across The Networker brand last year, more than 27,000 events were listed, and 17,000+ networkers used the directory to find a meeting, group, or opportunity.

Since then

Today

More support for organisers

As more groups used the directory, organisers needed tools beyond a listing. With Catherine leading the rebuild, The Networker UK grew into a ticketing platform and business opportunities network, with payouts, attendee tools, and a workspace for independent groups.

Members can still browse everything in one place. Organisers can manage listings, tickets, and enquiries from the same platform.
