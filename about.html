Our Story: From a Garden Tea to an Event Infrastructure

01

Born in a Garden Over a Cup of Tea

The Networker framework was originally founded by Sue and Rosie in the summer of 2023. Sitting in a garden comparing notes on the industry, they couldn’t believe how fragmented the landscape was. There wasn’t a single place that cleanly unified face-to-face meetings, online webinars, and large exhibitions across the UK. Realizing that a similar discovery roadblock existed for small businesses looking for funding, they took matters into their own hands and built the original The-Networker.co.uk—operating it as a massive, comprehensive business directory and regional marketplace.

02

The Next Generation: Redesigned for Infrastructure

As our community rapidly scaled, the operational demands of independent organisers grew far beyond what a static directory could handle. To support this expanding ecosystem, the platform underwent a complete technical and structural modernisation under the direction of Catherine.

Transitioning the platform away from a passive information hub, Catherine entirely re-engineered the architecture into a high-performance ticketing ecosystem and a targeted B2B opportunities network. Rebuilt from the ground up for speed, financial security, and seamless event execution, the modern Hub provides independent organisers with a robust, secure, and transactional workspace to manage their entire lifecycle, protect their ticket revenues, and scale their local chapters.

A Proud Family Enterprise

Today, The Networker Hub operates as a proud, multi-generational, family-owned business enterprise led by Rosie and incoming Operations Director, Catherine. By blending decades of grassroots community-building experience with rigorous modern software engineering, our leadership team provides an unmatched level of personal stability, accountability, and boots-on-the-ground support that automated, faceless corporate ticketing giants simply cannot replicate.

Our mission

We believe the right room at the right time changes careers and companies. The Networker Hub brings together event listings, organiser pages, and business opportunities in one trusted platform so members can find what matters locally and nationally, and organisers can reach the audiences they deserve.

The Networker Hub is operated by The Networker Group Ltd. We work with organisers across the UK to list quality networking and professional development opportunities.
