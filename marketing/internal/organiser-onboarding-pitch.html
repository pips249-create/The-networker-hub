Three problems every networking group faces

Generic platforms handle dates and payments. They don’t handle how membership-style networking actually works.

1 · Discovery

New people can’t find you unless they already know someone in the room. You’re competing with Eventbrite, LinkedIn, and word of mouth.

2 · Conversion

You offer trial visits, but your ticketing treats every booking the same. You can’t easily see who’s on visit one vs ready to join.

3 · Admin

Member rates via access codes, spreadsheets for check-in, WhatsApp for “who’s coming?” It works until it doesn’t.

Five tools generic platforms don’t have

Walk through each tool tied to their workflow. These are your strongest differentiators vs Eventbrite and Meetup.

🎫

Guest visit programme

Let newcomers try your room before they commit to paid membership.

  • 1–2 complimentary visits set on your organiser page
  • Paid member tickets unlock after visits are used
  • Opt out on specific dates (conferences, member-only evenings)
📊

Visit tracking

See who is still trialling vs ready to convert.

  • 1st visit vs returning labels on every attendee list
  • Filter and export CSV with visit metadata
  • Dashboard stats for new vs returning attendance
🪑

Category Exclusivity

Curate who is in the room before anyone pays.

  • Application-based attendance instead of open checkout
  • Industry, job title, and pitch — approve or deny from dashboard
  • Approved guests receive a payment link only
🎓

Previous Attendees

Reward past attendees with invite-only rates on repeat events.

  • Pull attendees from a past event and send locked invite links
  • Hidden previous attendee ticket — non-invited guests cannot access
  • Works alongside standard tickets for first-time visitors
📋

Membership register

Member-only ticket rates without access codes or manual lists.

  • Upload members individually or import CSV with expiry dates
  • Members only ticket — hidden from public, visible when signed in
  • Auto-notify members on new events · reports & booking reminders

Day-to-day extras: printable Avery name badges, booking confirmations and reminders sent automatically, team editor access, and CSV export for your CRM.

Handle objections

Click each objection to reveal the recommended response. Expand before a call if you want these on screen.

We’re fine on Eventbrite
Totally fair — it works for one-off events. The question is whether it handles guest visits, member-only tickets without codes, and visit tracking. Most groups list here alongside Eventbrite for a month and compare admin time and new bookings.
Our members won’t sign up for another platform
Browsing is free with no account. They only sign up when they book — about two minutes. Members on your list get invite emails and auto-notified on new events. Once signed in, member tickets appear automatically.
We manage membership in a CRM or spreadsheet
Keep that for renewals. Use our member register for booking and visibility — CSV import in minutes. Reports tell you who’s booked, who’s expiring, and who missed meetings.
What about our existing attendees?
Export from your current system, import to the member register. For repeat events, Previous Attendees lets you invite past bookers to loyalty rates. Run parallel for one cycle if needed.
We need category control in the room
Category Exclusivity is exactly that — applications before payment, approve or deny from dashboard, payment link only for approved guests.
What’s the catch on free listing?
No catch. We grow when organisers and members use the hub. Attendees pay a small booking fee at checkout. Optional paid boosts like Premium Spotlight if you want extra visibility.
