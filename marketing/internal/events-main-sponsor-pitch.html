Send these assets before the first day of your booked month. We handle upload, QA, and publishing.

Logo file

  • PNG or SVG with transparent background
  • Landscape format · min 400×120px
  • Readable at email width (~180px max)

Backlink URL

  • HTTPS homepage or campaign landing page
  • Opens in new tab from site and emails
  • Optional UTM: ?utm_source=networkerhub&utm_medium=sponsor
