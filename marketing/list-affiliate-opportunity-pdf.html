Plain-language product or service (not a raw tracking link). You share signup links after enquiry.

7 days to lifetime — partners know how long referrals are credited before they apply.

Who it suits

Optional — e.g. networkers with a local business audience — helps the right partners self-select.

Step by step

From listing to partner enquiry

  1. Open Business opportunities

    Sign in and choose List a listing. Select opportunity type Affiliate.

  2. Add programme details

    Title, summary, commission, what partners promote, cookie window, region and contact details. Keep claims factual.

  3. Submit free → pay after approval

    No charge to submit. When approved, start the £25 + VAT monthly subscription via Stripe. Optional Premium Spotlight (£55 one-time).

  4. Receive enquiries & onboard partners

    Enquiries land in your inbox and workspace. Vet partners, then share your Awin / Impact / own tracking links yourself.
