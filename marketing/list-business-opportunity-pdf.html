Step by step

From listing to enquiry in five steps

  1. Open the organiser dashboard

    Sign in at thenetworkeruk.com and go to Business opportunities in the sidebar. You need an organiser account — the same workspace used for events.

  2. Create your listing

    Choose List a listing and complete the form: title, opportunity type(s), summary, description, investment notes, location, industry, images and contact details. Add open days or talks if you host visits.

  3. Submit for review (no charge)

    Submit free of charge. You’ll get email updates when approved or if changes are needed. Edit anytime while pending.

  4. Pay after approval to go live

    When approved, you receive a Stripe link to start the monthly subscription (£25 + VAT). Your listing goes live on the directory immediately. Optional: add Premium Spotlight (£55 one-time, up to 30 days).

  5. Manage enquiries & open days

    Enquiries and open-day interest land in your workspace with email alerts. Reply directly, update details, or cancel the subscription anytime from your billing portal.
