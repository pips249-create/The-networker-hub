Step by step

From listing to enquiry in four steps

  1. Open the organiser dashboard

    Sign in at thenetworkeruk.com and go to Business opportunities in the sidebar. You need an organiser account, the same workspace used for events.

  2. Create your listing

    Choose List a listing and complete the form: title, opportunity type (franchise, partnership, side hustle, etc.), summary, description, investment notes, location, industry, images, and contact details.

  3. Submit & go live

    Submit for review. You'll receive email updates when approved or if changes are needed. Once live, your listing appears on the Business Opportunities browse page.

  4. Receive & manage enquiries

    When someone enquires, you get an email notification. Reply directly to the prospect and track enquiries from Business opportunities → Overview in your dashboard.

  5. Renew or update

    Listings run for your chosen term. You’ll get reminder emails before expiry. Renew or edit from your dashboard to stay visible.
