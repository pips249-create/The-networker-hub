Why use Member offers?

Random WhatsApp deals are easy to miss. Member offers keeps useful partner perks in one place — with clear codes, end dates, and who they are for.

When you claim the right offer before your next event cycle, the effects compound:

  • Less wasted spend on tools you abandon
  • Faster trial of services built for networkers
  • Clearer conversations with partners who “get” groups
The missing piece

Visibility and introductions already happen at events. Offers add the practical layer — trials, discounts, and help that make the next booking or follow-up easier.

Paid campaigns and cold DMs scale slowly. Here’s your four-step playbook for turning Member offers into quiet leverage.

Step 1

Think need first

Only claim what matches a real gap — a tool trial, a discount you will use this month, or help that shortens a task you already do.

Offer type What this means What to do
Free trial Sweet spot for testing Start here if you have not used the product yet
Discount code Workable if timed Claim when you already planned the purchase
1:1 help / clinic High value, limited seats Book if you have a clear question ready
Vague “message us” Too soft Skip unless the partner adds a real perk

Your claim formula:

  • Your stage
  • A concrete need
  • When you will use it
  • End date on the offer
◎

Exclusions matter too. If an offer is for organisers only — or a city you are not in — leave it for someone else.

Step 2
✦ Member zone

Claim cleanly

Open the offer, read the detail, copy the code if there is one, then use the outbound link before the end date.

1. Open the card

Check who it is for, the highlight badge, and any end date so you do not chase a closed perk.

2. Copy the code

If a promo code is listed, copy it once and keep the tab open until checkout confirms it.

3. Use the link

Partner links are tracked for fairness. Use the button on the offer — not a random Google result.

Step 3
✦ Member zone

Stack with events

The best offers multiply what you already do: prepare before a breakfast, follow up after an expo, or trial a tool while you are building a series of meetings.

Use offers as a multiplier
  • Before: trial a CRM or content tool
  • During: bring a discount you can mention
  • After: book the clinic while the need is fresh
