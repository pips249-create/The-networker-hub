Built for networking groups

Tools generic ticketing platforms don’t offer

These features are designed for membership-style business networking — trial visits before paid tickets, visit counts on your attendee list, and invite-only alumni rates on repeat events.

🎫

Guest visit programme

Offer 1–2 complimentary trial visits per newcomer (set on your organiser page). New attendees use free visits before member tickets unlock — or opt out on specific events like conferences.

📊

Visit tracking

Your attendee list shows 1st visit vs returning, visit counts per person, filters, and CSV export — so you know who is still trialling vs ready to convert to membership.

🪑

Category Exclusivity

Application-based attendance for curated rooms. Applicants share industry and job title; you approve before they pay — ideal when seat mix matters.

🎓

Alumni Fast-Pass

Invite past confirmed attendees to a hidden alumni ticket tier. Each invitee gets a locked booking link — no full-price fallback for non-invited guests.

Free to list. No monthly subscription for events. You receive the full ticket price you set; attendees pay one booking fee (4.5% + 20p per ticket) at checkout. Free events need no Stripe setup. Works alongside other platforms — many organisers list here as well as elsewhere.
