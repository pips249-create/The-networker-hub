Built for networking groups

Four tools built for membership-style networking

Generic ticketing handles dates and payments. These four features handle how UK networking groups actually grow — trial visits, conversion tracking, curated rooms, and repeat-event loyalty.

🎫

Guest visit programme

Let newcomers try your room before they commit to paid membership tickets.

  • Set 1–2 complimentary visits on your organiser page — applied across eligible events
  • New attendees book a free guest pass first; paid member tickets unlock after visits are used
  • Opt out on specific dates (conferences, member-only evenings) while keeping paid tickets live
  • Platform cap and organiser allowance work together so trial visits stay controlled
📊

Visit tracking

See who is still trialling vs ready to convert — on every attendee list.

  • 1st visit vs returning labels on bookings, with visit counts per person
  • Filter the attendee list by visit status before export or check-in
  • CSV export includes visit metadata for your CRM or membership follow-up
  • Dashboard stats surface new vs returning attendance at a glance
🪑

Category Exclusivity

Curate who is in the room before anyone pays — ideal when seat mix matters.

  • Switch an event to application-based attendance instead of open checkout
  • Applicants share industry, job title, and a short pitch; you approve or deny from the dashboard
  • Approved guests receive a payment link — declined applicants never see a buy button
  • Archived applications stay on record for repeat events and audit
🎓

Alumni Fast-Pass

Reward past attendees with invite-only rates on repeat conferences and annual events.

  • Pull confirmed attendees from a previous event and send locked alumni invite links
  • Each invitee books at the hidden alumni tier — non-invited guests cannot access the rate
  • Re-send safely: already invited addresses are not duplicated in your send queue
  • Works alongside standard tickets for first-time visitors who are not on the alumni list

Free to list. No monthly subscription for events. You receive the full ticket price you set; attendees pay one booking fee (4.5% + 20p per ticket) at checkout. Free events need no Stripe setup. Works alongside other platforms — many organisers list here as well as elsewhere.
