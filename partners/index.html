How it works

We approve a small number of referral partners. If we take you on, you get a unique link and code, a partner hub (earnings + media kit), and commission on qualifying sales.

  • What counts: business opportunity listings, Featured Opportunity Boost, and advertising & sponsorship packages
  • Commission: 20% of the sale ex-VAT — first 3 paid months on recurring; prepaid invoices and one-offs once
  • Paid: monthly after a 14-day hold · minimum payout £25
  • Not automatic: we review every application — this is not self-serve signup
