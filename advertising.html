How it works

1. Choose your section

Use the tabs above to pick Events, Organisers, or Opportunities — then email us the placement you want.

2. Vetting & creative

We review fit with our audience, confirm eligibility, and collect your logo, copy, and CTA link.

3. Go live

We publish your placement in Command Centre. Sponsored units are labelled clearly and monitored for ASA compliance.

Vetting & eligibility

  • All advertising partners are reviewed before publication — we only accept brands relevant to a professional business audience.
  • The Main Events Directory Sponsor is reserved for vetted third-party brands. Networking groups and event organisers cannot hold this slot.
  • Paid placements are labelled Sponsored or Sponsor Hub. See our advertising policy.
