Simple process

How it works

From first enquiry to go-live — every placement is reviewed, labelled clearly, and managed in Command Centre.

1. Choose your section

Use the options above to pick Events, Organisers, or Opportunities — then email us the placement you want.

2. Vetting & creative

We review fit with our audience, confirm eligibility, and collect your logo, copy, and link.

3. Go live

We publish your placement in Command Centre. All paid spots are labelled Sponsored and monitored for ASA compliance.

Vetting & eligibility

  • All advertising partners are reviewed before publication — we only accept brands relevant to a professional business audience.
  • The Main Events Directory Sponsor is reserved for vetted third-party brands. Networking groups and event organisers cannot hold this slot.
  • Main Sponsors appear on the browse page and in the email header of every email for that section (events, organisers, or opportunities).
  • Mini Sponsors appear on detail pages and in a Sponsored partners row on selected emails — not every send.
  • City Partners are website-only (not in emails). The same partner appears on matching events and opportunities city pages.
  • Paid placements are clearly labelled Sponsored on the website. See our advertising policy.
