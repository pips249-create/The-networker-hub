We’ve been quietly rebuilding The Networker

Hi,

We’ve been sitting on this for a while.

The Networker started as a UK directory so you didn’t have to hunt across five websites for a breakfast, a hall, or a group. Last year that meant…

27,000+

Events listed last year

17,000+

Networkers last year

But we knew we could do more for you all.

On 1st September we relaunch as The Networker UK — the UK platform for finding business events. Networking meetings, workshops, exhibitions, masterclasses, conferences, organiser pages, and business opportunities, all in one place, built for how you actually look for them.

The public site isn’t open yet. But we wanted to invite you to see the sneak peek.

What you can actually do on it

Find the next room worth walking into

Breakfasts, workshops, masterclasses, exhibitions, conferences — the UK business calendar, in one place, instead of a scavenger hunt.

Meet the group before you book

Read real reviews, guest visits, try a room, favourite the groups you like, and skip the ones that aren’t your people.

Find a new opportunity in your own time

Franchises, partnerships, side hustles and new ventures live on the Hub too. Browse when it suits you — no name badge, no breakfast required.
