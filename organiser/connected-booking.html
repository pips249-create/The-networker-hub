What is Connected booking?

List events on The Networker UK while buyers keep paying on Eventbrite, Ticket Tailor, or your website. We sync registrations so attendee lists, verified reviews, and round-ups still work — no monthly fee, and we do not take a cut of ticket sales. In your organiser workspace you will see this explanation first, then click Enable Connected — free when you are ready.

Prefer to sell tickets on The Networker UK instead? Hub checkout is free to list (4.5% + 20p booking fee per ticket). One model per event — you cannot mix Hub and Connected on the same listing.

  • Unlimited Connected event listings
  • Eventbrite, Ticket Tailor, or your own website

      Link a TNH event manually

      Use the TNH event UUID from Connected event setup, plus the id from your provider’s admin (Eventbrite numeric event id, Ticket Tailor ev_…, etc.).

      After each booking on your site, POST JSON to your webhook URL, for example: { "eventId": "TNH_EVENT_UUID", "orderId": "your-ref-123", "email": "buyer@example.com", "name": "Alex", "amountPaid": 15 }

      + View webhook & API documentation Endpoint, headers, and example JSON — for your developer or booking provider.
