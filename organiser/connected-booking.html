Connected booking

List events on The Networker UK, take payment on your own site, and sync registrations for verified reviews, attendee lists, and round-up emails.

Hub checkout vs Connected booking

Hub checkout (default) Connected booking
Listing Free From £39/mo (1 group) — see plans below
Where people pay The Networker UK Your website / booking system
Your cost Buyers pay 4.5% + 20p booking fee on top; you keep 100% of ticket price Monthly plan only — you keep 100% of ticket money
Verified post-event reviews Yes Yes (real-time webhook required)
Membership / guest visits / application seating Yes Not on Connected events — use Hub checkout

Webhook (for your developer)

When a ticket is sold on your site, POST JSON to /api/integrations/booking with header X-Networker-Signature: <HMAC-SHA256 of raw body> and X-Organiser-Account-Id: <your account id>.
