Connected booking

List events on The Networker UK, take payment on your own site, and sync registrations for verified reviews, attendee lists, and round-up emails.

Use Hub checkout instead if you want tickets, membership, and guest visits on The Networker UK (free listing; 4.5% + 20p booking fee per ticket).

Hub checkout vs Connected booking

Pick one model per event — you cannot mix both on the same listing.

Hub checkout (default) Connected booking
Listing on The Networker UK Free From £39/mo (see plans)
Where people pay On The Networker UK Your website / booking system
Your cost Buyers pay 4.5% + 20p booking fee; you keep 100% of ticket price Monthly plan only — you keep 100% of ticket money
Verified post-event reviews Yes Yes — real-time webhook required
Membership, guest visits, application seating Yes No — use Hub checkout for those events
