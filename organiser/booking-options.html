How booking options work

When you set up tickets, you pick one path. This page explains what each means for you and for the people who attend — so you can choose with confidence.

Ticket for this event Convert free visits into members Ticket and membership

Option 1

Ticket for this event

Pick this if you just want people to book this meeting — free or paid — with a simple ticket name and price.

What attendees see

Your ticket types on the event page (for example General admission £15, or Early bird £10). They book that ticket for that date.

What you set up

  • Ticket name, free or paid price, optional description
  • Optional cap on how many are available (leave blank for unlimited)

What you get

A clear guest list for that event. No monthly membership and no free-visit programme on this path.

Option 2

Convert free visits into members

Pick this if you run a regular group and want newcomers to try a few meetings, then join your membership — so you keep their visit history and reports in one place.

How the path works

  1. Free trial visits — newcomers can attend a few times without joining (you choose how many, up to 3).
  2. Member ticket for this meeting — people already on your member list book at your member price (often £0 when membership covers attendance).
  3. Monthly or yearly join fee — after free visits are used, newcomers pay to join your group. That fee is yours; we only add our booking fee on the join payment.

What attendees see

A chance to try your meetings first. When visits are used, a clear next step to join your group — not a surprise lock-in, and not “signing up to The Networker Hub” as a product. They are joining your group on the Hub.

What you get

  • Visit history for people who tried your meetings
  • A member list you can manage under Memberships
  • Reports on who came, who joined, and who is ready to pay

This path is about growing your community — free visits become members you can follow, not one-off guests who disappear.

Option 3

Ticket and membership

Pick this if some people should buy a normal ticket for this event, while regulars join (or already belong to) your group and book at a member rate.

What attendees see

Newcomers can buy a public ticket for this date. Members (or people converting after free visits) book the member rate — often free when membership already covers the meeting.

What you set up

  • Public ticket types for this event
  • Optional free trial visits and your monthly/yearly join fee
  • Member ticket price for list members (often £0)

What you get

Room for one-off guests and a path into membership with history and reports — useful when you want both open booking and a growing member base.

Not sure?

Most one-off or ticketed events use Ticket for this event. Most weekly networking groups that want a try-before-you-join path use Convert free visits into members.

You can change your mind before you publish. If you already have ticket sales on an event, some settings lock — Hubert or hello@thenetworkerhub.com can help.
