Option 1

Ticket for this event

Choose this if you just want people to book this date — free or paid. No membership.

  1. 1
    They pick a ticket

    A name and a price for this date — for example “Breakfast place, £15”.

  2. 2
    They book

    Paid tickets go through card checkout. Free tickets still reserve a place.

  3. 3
    You get a guest list

    Who is coming to this event. That is all this option does.

What attendees see

Your tickets on the event page. They pick one and book.

What you set up

  • Ticket name, free or paid price, optional description
  • Optional limit on how many are available (leave blank for unlimited)

What you get

A clear guest list for that event. No monthly membership and no free-visit programme on this path.

A typical example

Wednesday breakfast mixer — this date only

  • Everyone Breakfast place £15 Coffee and a seat for this morning. You can add a second ticket if you like, such as “bring a colleague”.

Names and prices are yours to set. This option is for a one-off booking — not a monthly group membership.

Option 2

Free visits, then membership

Choose this if you are starting a new group. People try a meeting free, then join. You do not need a guest ticket.

  1. 1
    Try a meeting free

    Newcomers attend a few times without joining (you choose how many, up to 3).

  2. 2
    Pay to join your group

    After visits are used, they pay a monthly or annual fee to become a member. That fee is yours; we only add our booking fee on the join payment.

  3. 3
    Then they book as a member

    Each later meeting is at your member price (often free). You stay on Option 2.

Do I stay on this option?

Yes. When someone joins, they book as a member next time. We do not switch you to Option 3.

What attendees see

For a new group. They try your meetings first. When visits are used, a clear next step to join your group so they can come regularly — not a surprise commitment, and not “signing up to The Networker Hub” as a product.

What you set up

  • How many free trial visits (up to 3)
  • Member ticket price for this meeting (often £0)
  • Monthly or annual membership fee after visits are used

What you get

  • Visit history for people who tried your meetings
  • A member list under Memberships
  • Reports on who came, who joined, and who is ready to pay

A typical example

Starting a Thursday breakfast group

  • Newcomer Free trial visits 2 free They try two breakfasts before they decide to join.
  • Then join Monthly membership £35 a month Join fee after free visits — covers ongoing meetings.
  • Member Each breakfast after that £0 Members book each meeting at no extra charge.

Prices are examples — you set your own. There is no separate guest ticket on this option. Visitors try free, then join. You do not move on to Option 3 later.

Benefits of running membership through the Hub

  • You keep 100% of the membership price you set — members pay a separate booking fee (4.5% + 20p) on top. That is not taken from your fee.
  • Join and renew without chasing — we invite people after free visits, take the card, and handle renewals. You manage the list under Memberships.
  • Visit history and reports — see who tried the group, who joined, who booked, and who is due to renew.
  • Your brand, your group — people join your membership on your organiser page, not a Hub subscription.
  • Invite to pay — ask people on your list to pay the join fee when you are ready, without chasing bank transfers by hand.
  • Reviews after they attend — attendees can leave a review once they have been to a meeting. Reviews show on your organiser page, help new visitors choose you, and you can reply from your dashboard.

Option 3

Ticket and membership

Choose this if you already have members. Guests buy a ticket. Members pay a different price, or nothing.

What attendees see

Guests buy a ticket for this date, or try a few meetings free then join. Your existing members skip the guest ticket and book at the member price (often free).

What you set up

  • A guest ticket (paid or free) for people who are not members
  • Member price so existing members can book (often free)
  • Optional free trial visits, then your monthly or annual membership fee

What you get

A way to bring in new guests and look after people already in the group — with visit history and reports in one place.

A typical example

Established Friday group — members already on the books

  • Guest Guest ticket £18 What a visitor pays for this breakfast (for example including lunch).
  • Existing member Member booking £0 People already in the group book at the member price — often free.
  • Optional Free trial visits, then join £40 a month New people can try two meetings free, then join to come regularly.

The difference from Option 2 is the guest ticket. Option 2 has no guest ticket — only try free, then join. Prices are examples — you set your own.

Membership fee handling, visit history, and “your brand, your group” work the same as Option 2 — see Benefits of running membership through the Hub.
