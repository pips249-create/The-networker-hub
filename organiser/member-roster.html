Member list

Your private list of members for this organiser page. People on it unlock Members only tickets when they sign in with their membership email. When you publish a new event, members on this list are emailed automatically.

How it works

  1. Add your members Upload a spreadsheet (CSV from Excel or Google Sheets) or add people one by one.
  2. Create a Members only ticket On your event’s tickets step, add a Members only tier — it stays off the public page.
  3. They get notified & book When you publish, members are emailed. Matching emails see the member rate when signed in.

Using member contact details

Use this list for membership and event booking only. Please do not add members to marketing lists or contact them about unrelated services without their consent.
