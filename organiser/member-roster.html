Your private list of members for this organiser page. People on it unlock Members only tickets when they sign in with their membership email.

How it works

  1. Add your members Upload a spreadsheet (CSV from Excel or Google Sheets) or add people one by one.
  2. Create a Members only ticket On your event’s tickets step, add a Members only tier — it stays off the public page.
  3. They sign in & book Matching emails see the member rate automatically. Optional expiry dates flag renewals in your reports.
