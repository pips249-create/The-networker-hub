Business opportunities

How listing works

Promote a franchise, side hustle, partnership, or ambassador role to Networker Hub members. They browse the directory, read your listing, and send you an enquiry — you reply by email.

  1. 1
    Add your listing details Title, opportunity type, investment, location, description, logo and cover photo.
  2. 2
    Pay the listing fee £20 per month + VAT, billed upfront for your chosen term (minimum 3 months). Secure payment via Stripe.
  3. 3
    Your listing goes live It appears in the business opportunities directory where members can filter by type and location.
  4. 4
    Respond to enquiries When someone is interested, their message appears in your organiser workspace under Enquiries received.
