How listing works

  1. 1
    Add your listing details Title, opportunity type, investment, location, description, logo and cover photo.
  2. 2
    Pay the listing fee £25 per month + VAT, billed upfront for your chosen term (minimum 3 months). Secure payment via Stripe.

    Your listing goes live It appears in the business opportunities directory where members can filter by type and location.
  4. 4
    Respond to enquiries When someone is interested, their message appears under Enquiries received in My business opportunities.
