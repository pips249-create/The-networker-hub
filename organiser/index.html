    i How membership works

    Members see the member rate when signed in with their membership email; everyone else never sees it. Add people here (or let them join and pay through the Hub), then set a Members only ticket on your event (Tickets step).

    Paid memberships: you receive 100% of the price you set; members pay a booking fee (4.5% + 20p) on top. Your membership VAT is separate. Bank details (Stripe Connect) are required before members can pay you. Changing the monthly or annual price only affects new joiners — existing subscribers keep their rate until they cancel and rejoin (use Invite to pay if you want them on the new price).

    Reports cover only people on your member list — not every event attendee (use Attendees for that). Members are emailed when you publish events and when you send booking reminders. Export CSV anytime for your own records.

    Use member contact details for membership and event booking only — not for unrelated marketing without consent.

    Top groups

    Monthly recognition for networking groups — ranked by attendee ratings, then review rate.

    Preview of how Top groups will look once groups earn badges

    Choose whether each group can be named on the public Top groups list and Hub LinkedIn shout-outs.

    How ranking is calculated
    1. Average rating first Groups are ordered by their average attendee star rating (highest first).
    2. Review rate breaks ties If two groups have the same average rating, the higher review rate ranks above.

    Review rate

    Number of reviews ÷ Past-event ticket buyers

    Ticket buyers = paid or free tickets on events that have already finished (approved bookings only; cancelled bookings do not count). A higher rate means a larger share of your past attendees left a review — so a smaller group with strong feedback can outrank a larger one with the same stars.

    • Groups need at least 3 reviews and a published organiser page to appear.
    • Badges (Top 10 / 25 / 50) refresh monthly from the latest snapshot. Website embeds only show an award when that group earned it for the month stamped on the badge.
