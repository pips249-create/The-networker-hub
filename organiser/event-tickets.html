Refund policy

Choose one of our fixed refund policies. Required before publishing.

Flexible 100% refund up to 48 hours before the event. Best for casual meetups with low catering costs.
Standard (recommended) 100% refund up to 7 days before the event. No refund after that.
Strict (B2B) 100% refund up to 14 days before the event. No refund after that — suited to conferences and catered events.
Non-refundable No change-of-mind refunds. Statutory rights still apply if you cancel or materially change the event.

Refunds and Stripe Connect

  • When attendees buy tickets, the ticket price is paid to your connected Stripe account at checkout
  • The attendee booking fee (4.5% + 20p per ticket) is retained by The Networker Hub — it is separate from your ticket revenue
  • Eligible refunds are processed through Stripe and debited from your connected account balance
  • “No refunds” only applies when an attendee cancels. You must still honour statutory rights, including refunds if you cancel or materially change the event
