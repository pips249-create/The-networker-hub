Refunds and Stripe Connect

  • When attendees buy tickets, the ticket price is paid to your connected Stripe account at checkout
  • The attendee booking fee (4.5% + 20p per ticket) is retained by The Networker Hub — it is separate from your ticket revenue
  • Refunds are processed through Stripe and debited from your connected account balance according to your refund policy
  • You must honour your refund policy and UK consumer law — including refunding all attendees within 14 days if you cancel the event

Your event is not live until you publish a ticket type — please add at least one ticket tier, choose how VAT applies, and complete the refund policy.
