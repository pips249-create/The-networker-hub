How the ranking works

  1. Highest average rating wins Groups are ordered by attendee star ratings first.
  2. Review rate breaks ties Reviews ÷ past-event ticket purchases — so smaller groups can still climb.
  3. Badges refresh monthly Top 10, Top 25 and Top 50 awards update from the latest snapshot.

To appear, a group needs a published profile, at least 8 reviews, and 10+ past-event ticket purchases.
