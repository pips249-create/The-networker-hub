    How markets launch

    We launch country by country. The UK is live today. Ireland and the USA are next. Canada, Mexico, and other markets stay on the map until demand justifies a local hub.

    What is The Networker International?
    The global routing layer for The Networker — choose your country to enter a live site, join a building market, browse worldwide link-out events, or register interest. Operated by The Networker Group Limited (Company No. 15252227).
    Is this the same as Networkers International recruitment?
    No. We are a business networking events platform — not the unrelated recruitment agencies at networkersint.co.za, Gattaca/Networkers UK recruitment, or networkers.com.eg IT training.
    Which markets are live or building?
    United Kingdom is live. Ireland and the United States are building. Canada, Mexico, and other countries are map-only for now; a few are unavailable for compliance reasons.
    Is there business networking in Ireland on The Networker?
    Ireland is building. Register interest or list your group. See the live UK site at thenetworkeruk.com meanwhile.
    Is The Networker live in Canada or Mexico?
    Not yet. Canada and Mexico each have their own map region for interest and event browse — they are not bundled into one North America hub. Use the world map to register interest or add an event.
    How are countries grouped on the map?
    Dedicated pages exist for the UK, Ireland, and the United States. Elsewhere we use tighter regions for browse and demand (for example Benelux, DACH, France, Iberia, Italy, Canada, Mexico) instead of one oversized Western Europe or North America group.
    How do I list my networking group?
    On Ireland or United States, choose “I want to list a group” and send your details. For other countries, use the map and choose list interest.
    Can I add an event outside the UK?
    Yes. Use Add your event to submit a link-out listing (title, date, place, organiser name, and booking URL). We review it before it appears under Events around the world. UK events should be listed on The Networker UK.
    How do markets launch?
    Country by country. Interest, group intake, and link-out events help us prioritise what follows the UK, Ireland, and the United States.
