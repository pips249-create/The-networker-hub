What is The Networker Hub?

A UK platform for discovering networking meetings, exhibitions, conferences, and business opportunities. Members can search and book; organisers can list events and reach new audiences.

Do I need an account to browse?

No — browsing is completely free. Explore events and business opportunities without signing in. You’ll need a free account to buy a ticket or enquire about an opportunity; sign-up takes about 2 minutes.

How do I book a ticket?

Create a free account first (about 2 minutes), then open an event, choose your ticket type, and complete secure Stripe checkout. Add guest names at checkout and view bookings in My tickets.

I’m an organiser — how do I list an event?

Sign in, claim your organiser page if prompted, then open the organiser dashboard to create events and manage bookings. Tools built for networking groups include guest visits, visit tracking, printable name badges, Category Exclusivity, and Alumni Fast-Pass. See our step-by-step guide or search the organiser directory to find your group.
