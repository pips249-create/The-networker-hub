What is The Networker Hub?

A UK platform for discovering networking meetings, exhibitions, conferences, and business opportunities. Networkers can search and book; organisers can list events and reach new audiences.

Do I need an account to browse?

No — browsing is completely free. Explore events and business opportunities without signing in. You’ll need a free account to buy a ticket or enquire about an opportunity; sign-up takes about 2 minutes.

How do I book a ticket?

Create a free account first (about 2 minutes), then open an event, choose your ticket type, and complete secure Stripe checkout. Add guest names at checkout and view bookings in My Hub.

I’m an organiser — how do I list an event?

Create a free account — we enable organiser access automatically. Confirm your email, then publish events or claim your page if your group is already listed. See what’s included for organisers · Sign in to your dashboard. The setup checklist and Hubert guide you through the rest.
